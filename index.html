Introducing Me...

Hello! My name is Mihika, I recently turned 20 and am in my junior year of university. I'm studying ATEC as a major and sociology as a minor and am close to having finished my minor! I had originally begun taking ATEC classes with graphic design in mind, and still I find myself most at ease with Adobe Photoshop just because of how long I’ve used it, but as time goes on I also find myself becoming very familiar with Figma and its versatility. As I took more specialized classes in ATEC, I found myself gravitating toward more research-heavy classes, and classes that are more focused on critical analysis and discussion. I love any environment where I get to share my opinion with parameters to explore it, as well as classes that create an environment for kind critique. Other than graphic design and media analysis, I love films and getting to dissect them- especially getting to make connections between whatever I may be learning in school to broader things I notice happening in my life. I also really enjoy reading, often personal or lyrical essays and poems and most commonly memoirs. I’ve in the past year forced myself to like hiking and broadly “the great outdoors,” and though it was an uncomfortable lifestyle change, I’ve come to have a greater appreciation for design, color, shape, and texture, because of how much more stimulation I receive. I also have a radio show through RadioUTD, and that’s given me a very unique creative outlet as someone who doesn’t have much musical prowess but absolutely loves it. It has been a way for me to experience forced exposure to programs and platforms that I found overwhelming, and the shows being “on air” has been a continuous lesson on playing by ear and self confidence. Creating posts for the show, curating weekly playlists and themes, and programming are all avenues of self expression that I uniquely have access to through university. I’ve always loved having a curated, unified aesthetic. Though that applies to my selection in clothes, jewelry, music, etc., that search for a consistent “look” came from my history doing graphic design. Personally, I’ve always been incredibly drawn to serif fonts and the Swiss style of design/ International Typographic Style, where the words are a part of the composition rather than resting on top- and individual, loud, details creating a vibrant but clean and discernible. I'm a maximalist in taste, but practically a minimalist, and I feel like that's most embodied and embraced by this design style. I've tried to implement those design principles in most everything I do, thinking about individual elements and how they harmonize with everything else, and trying to be intentional with placement. aesthetic. Though this class has been very daunting for me to approach, seeming at a glance completely antithetical to my skillset, I'm excited to become more literate and unlock a skillset I didn't previously have access to, especially because much of the design presented in class closely aligns with my personal taste.
